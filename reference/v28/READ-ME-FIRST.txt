START HERE
1. Open Slope_Calculation_Application_V28.html in Chrome or Microsoft Edge (index.html is the same file).
2. Select "Open application". Enter Y1, Y2, X1, X2 and the chainage interval in step 1. Results appear at the top right.

- 4 Crossfall / slope from centreline.
- 5 DIGITAL AUTO LEVEL SIMULATOR (new) - see below.
- 6 Optical level simulator (training animation, unchanged).
- 7 Dashboard of saved calculations.

DIGITAL AUTO LEVEL SIMULATOR (step 5)
- Uses your slope line (Y1, Y2, X1, X2, interval) as the ground.
- A digital level and barcode staff run along the line automatically: backsight (BS), intermediate sights (IS),
  change points (CP) and foresight (FS), using the HI method.
- LCD panel shows the rod reading to 0.1 mm, distance, HI and RL for each reading.
- Level book table: BS, IS, FS, HI, RL, slope RL, difference in mm, notes. Download it as CSV for Excel.
- Final check: arithmetic check (sum BS - sum FS = last RL - first RL) and closing error against design Y2
  compared with the allowable 12 sqrt(K) mm.
- Options: max sight distance, reading noise (mm), collimation error (mm per 100 m), speed.
  Buttons: Start / Pause, Step, Reset, New run (new random noise).
- It is a training and checking simulator. It does not connect to or reproduce any specific instrument model.

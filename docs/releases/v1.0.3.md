## Slope Calculator 1.0.3

**Sign in first.** Slope Calculator now opens on the sign-in, on Windows and on the web: use your
free Excel to KML account (the same one as exceltokml.com and Traverse & Level), or create one in a
minute with your email or Google. After the first sign-in the app remembers you and opens straight
away, offline too, until you sign out.

- Web app: if you are already signed in on exceltokml.com, it opens straight to the calculator.
- Saving offline: calculations saved without a connection wait on your computer and are sent to
  your account when you are back online ("Waiting to send", "Send now").

Installed copies offer this update a few seconds after they open. New here? Download
`SlopeCalculator-Setup.exe` below, or open the web app at
https://www.exceltokml.com/slope-calculation/app.

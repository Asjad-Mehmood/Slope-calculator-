## Slope Calculator 1.0.4

**Larger text, everywhere.** The app now follows your computer's or phone's text size (Windows
Settings → Accessibility → Text size, or the phone's font size), with A− / A+ on top. Every page
fits at up to twice the normal size, on a phone, a tablet or a large screen.

- The menu, the page buttons, Settings and the Project plate wrap or move under instead of
  running off the edge with large text.
- Excel reports store clean numbers (−0.3, not −0.299999999999997), so copied values and
  formulas give exactly what you see.
- Screen readers name each sign-in box by its label.
- New: the **user manual** at https://www.exceltokml.com/slope-calculation/manual (also as a PDF),
  and an updated **Excel template** that calculates the same slope, stations and crossfall with
  formulas.

Installed copies offer this update a few seconds after they open. New here? Download
`SlopeCalculator-Setup.exe` below, or open the web app at
https://www.exceltokml.com/slope-calculation/app.

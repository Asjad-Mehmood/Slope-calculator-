## Slope Calculator 1.0.0

The first release of Slope Calculator by Excel to KML: slope, gradient and chainage levels for
surveyors and site engineers.

**Download:** `SlopeCalculator-Setup.exe` below. It installs for your user only (no administrator
rights) and does not need Microsoft Edge WebView2. If SmartScreen says *Windows protected your PC*,
press **More info**, then **Run anyway**: this appears for programs without a paid signing
certificate.

### What it does
- Slope %, slope per metre, gradient 1 in N and the level at every chainage, with a profile and a
  probe to read any chainage.
- Project plate, crossfall with a cross-section, a digital level run booked by Height of
  Instrument or Rise & Fall and checked, and an optical level trainer.
- Excel report (with the level book), PDF report with sign-off boxes, print and CSV, all offline.
- Sign in with your Excel to KML account to keep calculations and open them on any device.
- Light and dark, adjustable text size. Updates itself: it asks when a newer version is out.
